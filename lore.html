A LITTLE CELESTIAL MATH
01

Near. Far. Just right.

The Sun is about 400× wider than the Moon—and about 400× farther away. From Earth, they appear almost exactly the same size.

D☉ / D☾ ≈ r☉ / r☾ ≈ 400
02

A narrow shadow.

The Moon's umbra traces a narrow path across Earth's surface. Outside this path, observers may see a partial eclipse instead.

UMBRA → TOTALITY → PENUMBRA
03

Not every month.

The Moon's orbit is tilted about 5° to Earth's orbit. An eclipse needs the Moon to cross an orbital node at just the right moment.

i ≈ 5.145° · SAROS ≈ 18 YEARS
